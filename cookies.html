The short version

This website does not set any cookies of its own, and it does not use analytics or advertising trackers. That is why you will not see a cookie banner here.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store in your browser. They are often used to remember settings, measure visits or show advertising.

When third parties may set cookies

  • Google Maps. The map in the contact section is switched off until you press Show map. If you choose to load it, Google may set cookies in line with the Google cookie policy.
  • External links. If you follow a link to WhatsApp, Facebook or Google Maps, those services may set their own cookies on their websites.

Local storage

The website does not use local storage, session storage or similar technologies to track you.

Managing cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not affect how this website works.
