Golf beneath the escarpment

A nine-hole, eighteen-tee bushveld course inside AFB Hoedspruit. Visitors are welcome every day of the year.

Book a tee time Explore the course

Drakensig is a nine-hole course inside the Drakensig estate at AFB Hoedspruit. Kikuyu fairways run between old bushveld trees, the greens are bent grass, and the Drakensberg escarpment fills the skyline from almost every tee.

Hoedspruit sits between the Kruger National Park and the Blyde River Canyon, and the course feels like part of that landscape. Expect wide fairways, shade under the trees and a clubhouse that looks straight down the eighteenth.

Shared with the locals

Impala graze the rough, warthogs work the edges of the greens and the dam is covered in waterlilies. The trees bring in exceptional birdlife all year.

Pack binoculars with your clubs, and give the animals right of way. They were here first.

Finish on the veranda

The clubhouse looks down the eighteenth towards the mountains. Stay for a cold drink after your round, or talk to us about hosting a function, year-end or conference.

Plan your round

The course sits inside a working Air Force base, so a little planning goes a long way. Here is what to know before you arrive.

When

Open all year
Monday to Sunday. Golf in the Lowveld is a twelve-month game.
Book ahead
Weekends and holidays fill up. Send a message to secure your tee time.
Beat the heat
Summer afternoons are hot. Early tee times are the most comfortable.

Getting here

Inside AFB Hoedspruit
The club is in the Drakensig residential estate on the Air Force base, just outside Hoedspruit town.
At the gate
Carry your ID and let security know you are playing golf. Let us know in advance if you are bringing a group.

On the course

Dress code
Smart casual. Collared shirts and golf or tailored shorts are always right.
Wildlife
Animals roam freely. Keep a respectful distance and never feed them.
