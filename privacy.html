Privacy Policy

How Drakensig Golf Club handles personal information, in line with POPIA.

Privacy Policy Terms of Use Cookie Policy

Who we are

This website belongs to Drakensig Golf Club, a golf club in the Drakensig Golf Estate at AFB Hoedspruit, Hoedspruit, Limpopo, 1380, South Africa. In terms of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), the club is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website.

Questions about privacy can be sent to our information officer at drakensigmilitarygolf@gmail.com or by phone on 066 014 2639.

What this website collects

This is a simple information website. It has no user accounts, no newsletter sign-up, no analytics and no advertising trackers. The website itself does not store anything you type.

When you use the booking or enquiry form, the details you enter (such as your name, phone number, preferred date, number of players and message) are placed into a WhatsApp message or an email on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp or your email app. Once you send it, the club receives the message like any other WhatsApp message or email.

Why we use your information

  • To respond to your tee time request or enquiry and confirm bookings.
  • To arrange functions, conferences and membership matters you ask about.
  • To share access information for AFB Hoedspruit where it is needed for your visit.
  • To meet legal, safety and security requirements that apply on the Air Force base.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

Your information is shared only where it is needed to help you, for example with base security when a visitor list is required for gate access, or where the law requires it. Messages you send travel through WhatsApp (Meta) or your email provider, whose own privacy policies apply.

Third-party services on this website

  • Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address in server logs to keep the service secure.
  • Icons. Interface icons load from the unpkg content delivery network, which receives standard technical request data.
  • Google Maps. The map in the contact section only loads after you press Show map. Once loaded, Google may set cookies and collect data under its own privacy policy.
  • Links. Links to Facebook, WhatsApp and Google Maps take you to services that have their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and enquiry messages only for as long as needed to deal with your request, manage club records, or as required by law. Messages are then deleted or archived securely.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information such as banking or ID numbers through the website form.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask whether we hold your personal information, request access to it, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its processing. Contact us using the details above and we will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

Children

This website is not aimed at children. Junior golfers are welcome at the club, but bookings for anyone under 18 should be made by a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
