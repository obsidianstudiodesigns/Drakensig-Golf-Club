Visiting AFB Hoedspruit

The course is located on an active South African Air Force base. Visitors must follow the instructions of base security, carry valid identification and comply with all access rules. Access to the base is at the discretion of the military authorities, and the club cannot guarantee entry.

  • Golfers play at their own risk. The club is not responsible for injury, loss or damage to persons or property, except where caused by the club's gross negligence.
  • Wild animals roam freely on the course. Keep a safe distance, never feed or approach animals, and give them right of way.

  • Golf carts must be driven responsibly and only on permitted areas.
